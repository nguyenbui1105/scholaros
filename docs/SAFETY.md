# Scholarship ethics and safety

- **Authenticity:** a student's story is theirs. The system may structure and coach, but must never invent awards, grades, obstacles, volunteer hours, hardship or financial circumstances.
- **AI restrictions:** scholarship-specific policy is binding. Unknown means no AI writing until verified. Editing-only allows edits only within confirmed terms; prohibited means no assistance restricted by those terms.
- **Eligibility:** don't assume F-1 eligibility based on marketing copy, vague "all students" wording or an HTTPS URL. A verified official rule is required.
- **Submission:** no unattended default auto-submit. Require exact final packet preview, explicit approval, provider allowance and a recorded submission confirmation.
- **Anti-abuse:** no circumventing CAPTCHAs, rate limits, anti-bot controls, authentication steps or provider restrictions.
- **Truthful metrics:** priority scores are not award chances. Real award rates need representative evidence and uncertainty estimates.
- **Sensitive data:** collect only necessary fields, enforce deletion/export, encrypt and isolate. Never store a user's actual story in example data.
- **Fairness:** audit systematic exclusion of visa categories, community colleges and non-native English writers. Avoid conflating fluency with merit.
